# Results: Artefacts, Schemas and Interpretation

## 1. Layout of `results/`

```
results/
├── <campaign>/
│   ├── seed_<n>.json        one file per seed: {"summary": {...}, "telemetry": [ ... ]}
│   ├── campaign.json        campaign-level configuration + per-seed summary index
│   ├── report.md            generated by experiments/write_report.py
│   └── figures/             accuracy_over_time.png, fault_timeline.png, pareto_bandwidth.png, summary_table.csv
├── transient_faults.json    output of experiments/transient_faults.py
└── *.log                    run logs (gitignored)
```
Campaigns (3 seeds, 72 hourly steps each, real orbit):

| Folder | Architecture | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `results/main_final/` | ResNet-18 | **Final** (2026-10-22, Colab T4, audit strategy included) |
| `results/main_final_mobilenet/` | MobileNetV2 | **Final**; contains `checkpoints/` (weights + held-out sidecar) |
| `results/main_gpu/` | ResNet-18 | **Pre-fix, do not cite**: predates the upstream-block T3 localisation fix and the CRC-audit strategy |
| `results/main/` | - | local scratch, may be empty |

Both final campaigns have exported frontend data (`frontend/public/data/*.json`).

## 2. `seed_<n>.json`

### `summary` (from `MissionSimulationHarness.get_campaign_summary`, plus `seed`, `wall_seconds`)

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `total_simulated_days`, `total_faults_accumulated`, `exponent_msb_flips_total` | Mission length, flips injected, flips on bit 30 |
| `fault_field`, `orbit`, `golden_int8`, `aegis_recalibrate` | Configuration that **must be quoted with any number** |
| `clean_accuracy` | Accuracy of the uncorrupted model on the evaluation set |
| `mean_accuracy`, `final_accuracy` | Dict by strategy key: `unmitigated, ranger_clipping, blink_recentering, periodic_scrubbing, aegis_v2, ranger_aegis, ground_uplink, aegis_audit` |
| `energy_consumed_uj` | Cumulative µJ by strategy (`periodic_scrubbing, aegis_v2, ranger_aegis, ground_uplink, aegis_audit`) plus `aegis_v2_monitor_share`, `aegis_v2_monitor_compute_share`, `energy_savings_percent` (vs 12 h scrub) |
| `bytes_transferred` | Cumulative bytes moved by repairing strategies |
| `aegis_restorations_count`, `ranger_aegis_restorations_count`, `ground_uplink_contacts_used`, `scrubs_executed`, `aegis_alarm_steps` | Event counts |
| `aegis_audit_hours`, `aegis_audits_executed` | Audit configuration and count |
| `saa_minutes_total` | SAA-core-equivalent minutes in the run |
| `golden_store_mb` | Compressed store sizes (`aegis`, `periodic_scrubbing`) |
| `scrub_sweep` | `{hours: {mean_accuracy, bytes_transferred, energy_consumed_uj}}` for the Pareto curve |

### `telemetry[i]` (one `TimeStepTelemetry` per step)

* Clock/orbit: `step_index, elapsed_minutes, elapsed_days, orbit_index, in_saa, saa_minutes_in_step`.
* Faults: `sbus_injected_step, mbus_injected_step, bit_flips_step, exponent_msb_flips_step, cumulative_faults`.
* Accuracy: `acc_unmitigated, acc_ranger, acc_blink, acc_periodic_scrub, acc_aegis, acc_ranger_aegis, acc_uplink, acc_aegis_audit`.
* Energy (µJ, cumulative): `energy_aegis_uj, energy_scrub_uj, energy_ranger_aegis_uj, energy_uplink_uj, energy_aegis_audit_uj,
  monitor_energy_aegis_uj, monitor_compute_energy_aegis_uj`.
* Bytes (cumulative): `bytes_aegis, bytes_scrub, bytes_ranger_aegis, bytes_uplink, bytes_aegis_audit`.
* Events: `scrub_executed, aegis_alarm, aegis_restored_blocks`.
* Sweep: `acc_scrub_sweep`, `bytes_scrub_sweep` (dicts keyed by hours).

Older campaigns omit newer columns; `aegis.reporting.strategies_present` handles that.

## 3. Reading `report.md`

1. **Setup** – architecture, seeds, orbit, SAA minutes/day, flip rates (mean ± interval), clean accuracy, store type.
2. **Accuracy, traffic and energy** – mean/final accuracy with 95 % Student-t intervals over seeds, MB/day, mJ/day.
   "not charged" marks strategies that move no bytes.
3. **Paired difference** – Aegis-v2 minus each baseline per seed, interval, and "seeds where Aegis-v2 is higher".
   Pairing is valid because every strategy sees the same fault stream.
4. **Bandwidth verdict** – whether any scrubbing interval *dominates* the strategy (higher accuracy at ≤ traffic) and
   how much traffic scrubbing needs to match it (linear interpolation between swept intervals).

## 4. Current numbers

### 4.1 `main_final` (ResNet-18, clean 89.3 %)
Setup: `--orbit tle`, 79.9 SAA-core-equivalent min/day, 147.1 ± 16.6 flips/day (4.89 ± 2.08 on the exponent MSB),
lossless store, no recalibration.

| Strategy | Mean acc. (%) | Final acc. (%) | MB/day | Energy (mJ/day) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aegis-v2 selective restore | 87.4 ± 8.3 | 83.6 ± 24.9 | 188.1 | 144.1 ± 35.8 |
| Aegis-v2 + CRC audit | 88.9 ± 1.7 | 89.3 ± 0.0 | 279.6 | 202.3 ± 20.3 |
| Ranger + Aegis-v2 | 87.4 ± 8.3 | 83.6 ± 24.9 | 143.0 | 120.5 ± 35.6 |
| Periodic scrubbing (12 h) | 37.0 ± 27.0 | 89.3 ± 0.0 | 83.2 | 27.9 |
| Ground uplink reload | 66.2 ± 10.2 | 52.4 ± 91.4 | 121.5 | 634.5 ± 167.3 |
| Ranger clipping | 38.5 ± 56.4 | 27.1 ± 43.1 | 0 | not charged |
| BLINK re-centering | 34.0 ± 42.0 | 13.6 ± 16.8 | 0 | not charged |
| Unmitigated | 14.0 ± 9.8 | 10.0 ± 0.0 | 0 | not charged |
| Scrub 1 h / 3 h / 6 h / 24 h | 89.3 / 77.6 / 66.4 / 22.0 | n/a | 998 / 333 / 166 / 41.6 | 334.6 / 111.5 / 55.8 / 13.9 |

Paired Aegis-v2 minus baseline (mean accuracy, points): vs audit -1.5 ± 6.6, vs Ranger+Aegis 0.0, vs 12 h scrub
+50.5 ± 23.6 (3/3 seeds), vs ground uplink +21.2 ± 5.3 (3/3), vs Ranger +48.9 ± 48.2 (3/3), vs BLINK +53.4 ± 36.4
(3/3), vs unmitigated +73.4 ± 4.8 (3/3).

Bandwidth verdict: Aegis-v2 is not dominated by any scrub interval; scrubbing needs about 889 MB/day to match it (4.73x).
Audit variant: 88.9 % at 279.6 MB/day, scrub needs about 975 MB/day (3.49x). Ranger+Aegis: 6.22x.

### 4.2 `main_final_mobilenet` (MobileNetV2, clean 99.3 %, held-out split verified)
Setup: same orbit, but only 29.1 ± 0.5 flips/day (1.11 ± 0.96 exponent-MSB) because the model has far fewer stored bits.

| Strategy | Mean acc. (%) | Final acc. (%) | MB/day | Energy (mJ/day) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aegis-v2 selective restore | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 3.4 | 125.6 ± 2.5 |
| Aegis-v2 + CRC audit | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 18.1 | 136.4 ± 2.5 |
| Ranger + Aegis-v2 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 1.7 | 124.7 ± 1.0 |
| Periodic scrubbing (12 h) | 87.2 ± 26.1 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 16.9 | 5.7 |
| Ground uplink reload | 93.3 ± 12.9 | 99.3 ± 0.0 | 3.5 | 141.5 ± 27.7 |
| Ranger clipping | 98.8 ± 1.4 | 96.7 ± 6.0 | 0 | not charged |
| BLINK re-centering | 79.5 ± 17.1 | 60.4 ± 44.9 | 0 | not charged |
| Unmitigated | 76.9 ± 51.7 | 39.8 ± 128.1 | 0 | not charged |
| Scrub 1 h / 3 h / 6 h / 24 h | 99.3 / 97.3 / 96.4 / 85.1 | n/a | 202 / 67.5 / 33.7 / 8.4 | 67.9 / 22.6 / 11.3 / 2.8 |

Paired deltas are small and inconclusive: vs 12 h scrub +12.1 ± 26.1 (2/3), vs uplink +6.0 ± 12.9 (2/3), vs Ranger
+0.5 ± 1.4 (2/3), vs BLINK +19.8 ± 17.1 (3/3). **Seed 2 had no damaging fault**, so it carries little information.
Scrubbing needs about 202 MB/day to match Aegis-v2 (about 60x), a large ratio mainly because Aegis-v2 moves almost
nothing (3.4 MB/day).

### 4.3 How to read these honestly
* **Supported:** Aegis-v2 holds near-clean accuracy at a fraction of the traffic that scrubbing needs; it beats
  Ranger, BLINK, uplink and unmitigated on ResNet-18 in all 3 seeds.
* **Not supported: energy savings.** Monitor hook compute makes Aegis-v2 about 125-144 mJ/day versus about 6-28 mJ/day
  for 12 h scrubbing. The claim is **bandwidth, not energy**.
* **Audit trade-off:** the CRC audit tightens ResNet-18 results (88.9 ± 1.7 %, final 89.3 %) for about 49 % more traffic
  and about 40 % more energy; the difference versus plain Aegis-v2 is within the intervals.
* **Ranger+Aegis equals Aegis-v2 in accuracy** here (identical means), with lower traffic; do not claim an accuracy gain.
* **Final accuracy** is one end-of-run snapshot and is very noisy (intervals up to ±128 pts); prefer mean accuracy.
* **Small samples:** 3 seeds and 150 evaluation chips (1 chip is about 0.67 pt) give wide intervals.
* Never cite pre-2026-10-21 numbers (e.g. "98.9 % energy savings") or `main_gpu`.

## 5. `transient_faults.json`
Result of the transient activation-fault experiment: spurious restores = **0 %** (T3 confirms no stored-bit change, so a
transient glitch cannot trigger a restore). Parameters: trials, bit (30 = exponent MSB), batch, seed.
